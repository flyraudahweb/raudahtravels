Starlink Deployment Proposal

Letter of Presentation

Dear Sir,

We are pleased to submit this proposal for the deployment of a Starlink broadband network and a robust Local Area Network (LAN) architecture at the Rimi Local Government Secretariat.

Effective administration in the modern era relies heavily on seamless digital communication. We have observed that the current lack of a reliable, high-speed network at the secretariat significantly stifles productivity, creates information silos, and slows down essential government operations.

By integrating Starlink's cutting-edge satellite internet with high-capacity indoor and outdoor access points, we will eliminate connectivity dead zones and equip your administrative departments with the infrastructure required for instant e-governance and efficient public service delivery.

We are confident that this technological upliftment will transform the secretariat's daily operations. We look forward to partnering with Rimi Local Government to bring this vision to life.

Sincerely,

Aliyu Wada Umar
